## User-facing change

Money supports All/Day/Week/Month/Year/Custom local ranges, chronological
history ordering, and Android OS date/time dialogs for local money and task
date/time fields. App Time derives daily average, active-app count, top-app
share, busiest local day, and previous-equivalent-period change from included
Usage Access snapshots, with explicit denied and empty states.
