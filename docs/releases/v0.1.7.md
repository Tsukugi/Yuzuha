## User-facing change

Settings Appearance keeps System, Light, and Dark contrast modes and now offers
five color palettes: Moss, Ocean, Sunset, Plum, and Citrus. Each palette has
light and dark values and is applied through the shared theme provider across
the shell, charts, forms, lists, and secondary screens.

The five idea cards in [`docs/theme-ideas/`](../theme-ideas/) document the
directions used by the selectors. Palette and contrast choices remain view
state and reset on relaunch; they do not change AppData or export formats.
